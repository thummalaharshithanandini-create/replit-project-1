# AI COOKING VIDEO GENERATION

Add an **"AI Cooking Video"** feature to every generated recipe.

The video should demonstrate how to prepare the selected recipe using the **same ingredients that the user provided**.

Example:

User enters:

* Tomato
* Onion
* Potato
* Green Chilli
* Salt
* Oil

The application recommends:

**Potato Tomato Curry**

When the user clicks:

🎥 **Watch AI Cooking Video**

Generate or display a short cooking video demonstrating:

1. The user's available ingredients.
2. Preparing/chopping the ingredients.
3. Cooking the ingredients.
4. Adding spices/seasoning.
5. Final cooking process.
6. Plating the finished dish.

The video should follow the selected recipe and should NOT randomly demonstrate unrelated ingredients.

---

## VIDEO PROMPT GENERATION

Create an AI video prompt automatically from:

* Recipe name
* User's available ingredients
* Missing ingredients
* Cooking steps
* Cuisine
* Cooking method

Example generated internal prompt:

"Create a realistic step-by-step cooking video demonstrating Potato Tomato Curry using potato, tomato, onion, green chilli, salt and oil. Show ingredient preparation, chopping, sautéing, cooking and final plating. Use a clean home kitchen environment and realistic food preparation."

If the user changes ingredients, generate a recipe/video context based on the new ingredients.

---

## VIDEO FALLBACK

Do NOT make the entire application dependent on an AI video API.

If an AI video-generation service is unavailable:

* Show a recipe step-by-step visual cooking guide.
* Use recipe-related images/animations.
* Display a "Video generation unavailable" friendly message.
* Keep the complete recipe functionality working.

Do not show a broken video player.

---

## VIDEO UI

Inside the recipe details page add:

🎥 **Watch Cooking Video**

and optionally:

✨ **Generate AI Cooking Video**

Show:

* Video preview
* Play/Pause
* Fullscreen
* Recipe name
* Ingredients used
* Cooking duration

Keep the video section visually prominent but don't make it mandatory for using the recipe.
